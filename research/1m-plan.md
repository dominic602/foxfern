# Fox & Fern: The $1M Plan

Prepared 2026-10-27. Team: Dominic (owner), 1 project manager, 1 salesperson, 2 sub crews. Budget: $4K/month paid ads.

All job prices, durations, close rates and lead costs below are **planning assumptions**. Replace them with your real Housecall Pro numbers after the first 90 days.

---

## 1. The target in one line

**Sell ~$85K a month (~$20K a week), which is about 3–4 major jobs plus 5–6 small jobs a month.**

## 2. Revenue mix

| Line | Jobs / year | Avg ticket | Revenue | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bathrooms | 30 | $15,000 | $450,000 | Your lead product ($5K+ start, $300 credit, $149/mo) |
| Kitchens | 8 | $40,000 | $320,000 | Biggest lever. Each kitchen = 2–3 baths of revenue |
| Basements | 3 | $40,000 | $120,000 | Good winter work |
| Repairs, rental turnovers, property mgmt, small jobs | ~70 | ~$1,500 | $110,000 | Fills crew gaps, feeds referrals |
| **Total** | **41 major + ~70 small** | **~$21.7K avg major** | **$1,000,000** | |

**Mix rule:** every kitchen you sell is worth 2–3 bathrooms. Moving 4 jobs a year from baths to kitchens adds ~$100K without more leads.

## 3. Crew capacity check

| Work | Crew-weeks needed (est.) |
|---|---|
| 30 baths × ~2 weeks | 60 |
| 8 kitchens × ~4 weeks | 32 |
| 3 basements × ~5 weeks | 15 |
| **Total** | **~107 crew-weeks** |
| **Available: 2 crews × 48 weeks** | **96 crew-weeks** |

**Two crews are ~10% short.** Fix it with one of these:
- **Overlap trades.** The plumber and electrician rough-in while the crew starts the next job. This shortens each job.
- **A separate handyman / repair sub** for the ~70 small jobs, so the 2 main crews stay on remodels.
- **A third sub crew from Q2 2027** once backlog stays above 6 weeks.

## 7. Money

| | Target |
|---|---|
| Gross margin (price every job for it) | **33%+** (NAHB average is 29.9%) |
| Gross profit at $1M | ~$330K |
| Marketing | ~$54K (5.4%) |
| Net profit after salaries and overhead | Aim for **8–10%** (NAHB average 6.3%) |

**Cash flow:** collect deposits and progress payments on every job. **The Pennsylvania Home Improvement Consumer Protection Act limits deposits to one-third of the contract price plus the cost of special-order materials**, so set up the payment schedule accordingly. Financing through Wisetack pays you while the customer pays monthly.

**Sales pay:** tie the salesperson's commission to **gross profit**, not revenue, so discounts come out of their pay too.

## 8. Weekly scoreboard (every Monday, 15 minutes)

| Number | Weekly target |
|---|---|
| New leads (by source) | 6–8 |
| Estimates sent | 3+ |
| $ sold | **$20K** |
| Backlog per crew | **4–6 weeks** |
| Reviews collected | 1–2 |

**Rules:**
- Backlog **under 3 weeks** → call old estimates, push property managers, increase ad spend.
- Backlog **over 8 weeks** → raise prices or add a crew.

## 9. Habits that matter most
1. **Call new leads within 5 minutes.**
2. **Send the estimate within 48 hours.**
3. **Follow up 5 times:** days 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30.
4. **Present financing on every estimate.**
5. **Ask for a review on every job.**
6. **Tag every lead's source** in Housecall Pro.
