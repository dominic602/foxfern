## 6. Negative keywords (in `google-ads-negatives.csv`)

DIY/ideas/pictures/how-to · jobs/salary/hiring · Home Depot/Lowe's/IKEA · **competitor brands** (Bath Fitter, Re-Bath, West Shore Home, Jacuzzi, Luxury Bath) · mobile home/RV · repair-only terms (plumber, leak, clogged, faucet repair) · showroom/wholesale/supply.

**Deliberately not negated:**
- **"free"**: it would block "free estimate" searches.
- **"cheap"**: it fits a $5,000 starting price. Watch lead quality for a few weeks.
- **"rental" and "landlord"**: you do rental work.

**Optional test:** a separate small campaign *bidding on* "bath fitter cost" / "bath fitter alternative", with ad copy like "Custom remodel from $5,000, not a liner". Don't use competitor brand names in the ad text.
