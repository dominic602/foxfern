## 2. Basement finishing

**Pricing benchmarks:**
- YourHomePro (local): standard finish **$40,000–$70,000** (3–6 weeks); bedroom + bath + office $70,000–$100,000; in-law suite / theater / bar $100,000+.
- Sweeten (Philadelphia): cosmetic from ~$50/sq ft (~$30,000 for 600 sq ft); egress window adds ~$10,000.
- 2025 Cost vs. Value (Philadelphia region): basement remodel **$52,199**, 65% recouped at resale.

**Permits:** Montgomery Township's packet says basement finishing **requires a permit**, usually plus mechanical, electrical and plumbing permits, and a bedroom needs an **egress** (emergency escape) opening. Good FAQ material that builds trust.

## 6. What to build, in order

Right now `/residential-remodeling/` covers kitchens, baths, basements, whole-home, painting, roofing, siding, stucco, masonry, decks and concrete **on one URL**. Google can't rank one page for all of those.

1. **`/kitchen-remodeling/`**: indexed service page. Target the Kitchen "Core" keywords and add sections for cabinets, layout and cost.
2. **`/basement-finishing/`**: same idea, with permits/egress and in-law suite sections. Use your Philadelphia epoxy-floor basement project.
3. **`/whole-home-renovation/`**: your homepage animation already tells this story. Add "renovating before you move in" and older-home sections.
4. **Cost guides**, in the format of your painting post: kitchen → basement → whole-home. Every competitor that ranks publishes 2025–2026 cost guides.
5. **422-corridor town pages** covering all services: Collegeville/Trappe → Royersford → Limerick/Linfield → Pottstown. Each needs real local projects and photos.
6. **Ad landing pages** for kitchen and basement, cloned from the bathroom page (form, calculator, offer stack).
7. **Google Business Profile:** add the categories Kitchen remodeler, Basement remodeler and General contractor, with photos per service.
