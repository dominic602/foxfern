Built right behind the tile

A beautiful bathroom that stays beautiful

Most bathroom failures start where you can't see them. Here's what we get right before a single tile goes up:

  1. Waterproofing firstA waterproof membrane goes behind every shower wall. Tile and grout alone aren't waterproof.
  2. Venting outsideThe exhaust fan is sized to the room and ducted outdoors, not into the attic.
  3. Fixtures chosen earlyShower valves go in the wall before tile, so we settle trim choices with you up front and avoid delays.
  4. Inspected before the walls closePlumbing and electrical rough-ins pass inspection before drywall and tile.

Tip: ask every contractor you're comparing which membrane goes behind the shower walls. It tells you a lot.

Questions

Bathroom remodeling questions

Is the estimate really free? What's the $300 credit?

Yes. We come out, walk the bathroom with you, take measurements and send a written, itemized estimate at no charge and with no obligation. If you go ahead, we take $300 off your fixtures: faucet, shower trim, lighting or hardware. See offer terms.

How much does a bathroom remodel cost?

Bathroom remodels start at $5,000. Your price depends on the size of the room, whether plumbing moves, and the tile, vanity and fixtures you choose. Your estimate lists every item at a fixed price, so you can see exactly where the money goes and adjust before anything starts. Requests from this page also get a free $300 fixture credit.

Will the price change once you start?

Not without your signature. The price in your estimate only changes if you approve a written change order.

How long will my bathroom be out of commission?

Your estimate includes the schedule for your specific project. Once work starts, your project app shows what was done each day and what's next, so you always know where things stand.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. We pull the permits your township requires and schedule the inspections. Plumbing and electrical rough-ins are inspected before the walls close. If you have an HOA, we help with that approval too.

Can I finance my bathroom remodel?

Yes. Through Wisetack, eligible homeowners can get 0% APR for 12 months* or a longer term with lower payments, as low as $149/month on a $5,000 bathroom.† Checking your options is a soft credit check and won't affect your credit score. Use the payment calculator, then tick the financing box in the form.

What warranty do I get?

At closeout you receive a warranty certificate for our workmanship (the term is set in your contract) and a product sheet listing the manufacturer, model and finish of everything we installed, so manufacturer warranties are easy to use.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Linfield and remodel bathrooms across Montgomery County, including Limerick, Royersford, Pottstown, Collegeville, King of Prussia, Conshohocken, Willow Grove and Bryn Mawr, plus Philadelphia and Berks County towns like Boyertown.
