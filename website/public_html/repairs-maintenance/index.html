Plumbing Repairs

Plumbing repairs in Limerick, Royersford and nearby: leaks, faucets, toilets, shutoff valves and water heaters, with the price approved before we start.

  • Leaks and pipe repairs
  • Faucets, toilets and fixtures
  • Shutoff valves and supply lines
  • Water heater replacement
Planning guide: plumbing repairs
  • Find your main shutoff now. In most homes it's in the basement where the water line comes through the wall. Knowing where it is turns a flood into a puddle.
  • Small drips add up. A running toilet or dripping faucet wastes water around the clock and usually means a worn part that's cheap to replace.
  • Water heaters give warning signs. Rust-colored hot water, rumbling noises or a damp base are worth a look before the tank fails.

Electrical Repairs

Outlets, switches, lighting and ceiling fans for homes and businesses across Montgomery County, with permits and inspections where your township requires them.

  • Outlets, switches and GFCI protection
  • Light fixtures and ceiling fans
  • LED lighting upgrades
  • Dead outlets and tripping breakers
Planning guide: electrical repairs
  • GFCI outlets protect wet areas. Kitchens, bathrooms, laundry areas, garages and outdoor outlets should have them. Press TEST, then RESET, once a month.
  • A breaker that keeps tripping is a warning. The circuit is overloaded or has a fault. Resetting it again and again isn't a fix.
  • Warm or buzzing outlets need a look. Discoloration, flickering or a burning smell mean turn off that circuit and call.

Wood Rot Repair & Replacement

Rotted trim, fascia, soffits, window and door frames, porch boards and deck parts cut out and replaced with matching material, with the water that caused it stopped first so the rot doesn't come back.

  • Fascia, soffit & exterior trim replacement
  • Window & door frame repair
  • Deck & porch board repair
  • Structural repair and sistering of framing

Exterior carpentry from recent jobs: new trim, soffits, gutters and downspouts with the Boyertown siding, trim repainted and a new porch roof in the King of Prussia stucco restoration, and composite decks in Pottstown and Royersford.

Planning guide: wood rot
  • Find the source first. Rot needs water. A failed gutter, missing flashing, open caulk joint or splash-back from a hard surface is usually behind it, and it gets fixed in the same visit.
  • Repair or replace? Sound wood with a small soft spot can be cut back and filled with an epoxy repair. Wood that's soft through, or that carries weight, gets replaced.
  • Matched, not patched. The new piece matches the profile, species and dimensions of the old one, then is primed and painted, so the repair is hard to find once it's done.
  • Rot-resistant options. For trim and fascia that stay wet, PVC or fiber-cement trim and composite decking won't rot again. We'll tell you where they make sense and where real wood is the better match.

What to Do First When Something Breaks

Pick what's happening to see the first steps, how urgent it is and who to call.

What's happening?

Emergency · Leave now

You smell gas

  1. Get everyone out of the building now.
  2. Don't flip switches, use a phone or light anything inside.
  3. Once you're outside and away from the building, call 911 or your gas utility.

Gas leaks are handled by the gas utility. Once they've made it safe, we can repair any damage.

Urgent · Call us

Water is leaking right now

  1. Close the shutoff valve under the fixture. If there isn't one, close the main water shutoff.
  2. If water is near outlets, lights or the electrical panel, stay out of the water and keep away from them.
  3. Move belongings out of the way and take photos for your insurance.

Then call (610) 890-3537 and we'll schedule the repair.

Urgent · Call us

Sparks or a burning smell

  1. If you see smoke or flames, get out and call 911.
  2. Otherwise, switch off that circuit's breaker if you can reach the panel safely.
  3. Don't use the outlet or fixture until it's been checked.

Then call (610) 890-3537 and we'll schedule the repair.

Urgent · Call us

No water and it's below freezing

  1. A pipe may be frozen. Leave the faucet open so water can flow as it thaws.
  2. Warm the area with a hair dryer or a space heater kept away from anything that can burn. Never use an open flame.
  3. If a pipe has burst, close the main water shutoff right away.

Then call (610) 890-3537 so we can repair the pipe and protect it from the next freeze.

Urgent · Call us

The roof is leaking in a storm

  1. Catch the water in buckets and move belongings away from the drip.
  2. If the ceiling is sagging or bulging with water, keep everyone out from under it.
  3. Stay off the roof. Take photos of the leak and any damage for your insurance.

Then call (610) 890-3537. We'll schedule the repair once it's safe to work on the roof.

Check first

Power is out in one room

  1. Look in the panel for a breaker that has tripped to the middle or OFF position, and switch it fully off, then on.
  2. Press RESET on nearby GFCI outlets. One GFCI can cut power to other outlets on the same circuit.
  3. If the breaker trips again, leave it off.

Still out, or tripping again? Request a repair.

Can be scheduled

A dripping faucet or running toilet

  1. It's not an emergency, but it wastes water every hour it runs.
  2. If the drip is heavy, close the shutoff valve under the fixture.
  3. Note the brand of the fixture if you can see it; it helps us bring the right parts.

Request a repair and add anything else on your list.

A hole or crack in a wall

  1. Most holes and hairline cracks aren't urgent.
  2. A brown stain or soft drywall can mean a hidden leak, so mention it when you contact us.
  3. Cracks that keep growing, or doors that suddenly stick, are worth a closer look.

Request a repair with a photo if you can.

Questions

Repair & Maintenance Questions

Do you take on small repairs?

Yes. Send us your whole list. Grouping small repairs into one visit is the most efficient way to get them done, and you approve the price for each item before we start.

Do you handle plumbing and electrical repairs?

Yes. Plumbing and electrical repairs are done by qualified tradespeople, with permits and inspections wherever your municipality requires them.

How is a repair priced?

You get an itemized price before work starts. If we find something else once we're on site, we photograph it and price it separately. Nothing is added without your OK.

What's included in a maintenance agreement?

A set visit calendar, the same checklist at every visit with notes and photos, and a list of recommended repairs, each priced separately. The visit schedule and checklist are set for your property after a walkthrough.

Can you handle turnovers across several properties?

Yes. You get one point of contact for your portfolio, and every work order, photo and invoice is filed by property and unit.

When does a repair become a remodel?

When the same thing keeps breaking, like a tub that keeps leaking or a roof patched every year, replacing it is often the better value. We'll tell you when we see it. Our residential remodeling page covers kitchens, baths, roofing and more.

Can I finance a home repair?

Homeowners can apply for monthly payments through Wisetack on qualifying projects. See financing.*
