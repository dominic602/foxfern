Primary & hall baths

Full Bathroom Remodels

Down to the studs or a smart refresh: new layout, tile, vanity, lighting and ventilation for primary and hall baths in Trappe.

  • Layout changes & plumbing moves
  • Tile floors, walls & showers
  • Vanities, mirrors & lighting
  • Exhaust fans vented outdoors
Planning guide: full remodels
  • Settle the layout first. Moving a toilet, shower or sink changes plumbing and permit needs more than any finish choice.
  • Choose the shower valve before tile. The valve goes in the wall at rough-in, so trim style and finish have to be decided early.
  • Plan your other bathroom. We map out which bathroom you'll use while yours is torn out, and keep the path to it protected.
  • Order special items early. Custom vanities, glass doors and some tile take weeks, so we order as soon as you approve.

Showers

Tub-to-Shower Conversions & Walk-In Showers

Trade a tub you never use for a walk-in shower with a low threshold, a built-in niche, blocking for grab bars and a glass door.

  • Tub removal & new shower pan
  • Cement board & waterproof membrane
  • Niches, benches & grab-bar blocking
  • Glass doors with chrome, black or brass trim
Planning guide: tub-to-shower
  • Keep one tub in the house. If you have young kids or plan to sell, a tub in the hall bath keeps the house appealing to more buyers.
  • Low threshold or curbless? A curbless shower needs the floor recessed, which is easier during a full remodel.
  • Add blocking now. Wood blocking behind the tile lets you add grab bars later without hunting for studs.
  • Moving the drain? In Trappe that usually means a plumbing permit, which we file for you.

Tile

Tile Showers & Tub Surrounds

Stacked, subway, large-format or mosaic tile, set over a waterproofed wall so it looks sharp and stays dry for years.

  • Wall & floor tile
  • Niches & trim pieces
  • Grout color & sealing
  • Tub replacement
Planning guide: tile
  • Waterproofing matters more than tile. Ask any contractor what membrane goes behind the shower walls.
  • Big tiles need flat walls. Large-format tile shows every bump, so walls get flattened before it goes up.
  • Darker grout hides wear. Charcoal or black grout keeps a white shower looking clean longer.
  • Keep a spare box. We order 10–15% extra and leave the leftovers labeled for future repairs.

Powder rooms

Powder Room Makeovers

A small room that makes a big first impression: wallpaper, wainscoting, a new vanity, faucet and toilet. A quick project when the plumbing stays put.

  • Wallpaper & wainscoting
  • Vanity, faucet & toilet
  • Lighting & mirrors
  • Floor tile & trim
Planning guide: powder rooms
  • Go bold here. With no shower steam, a powder room can take wallpaper and colors you'd skip in a full bath.
  • Match your metals. Pick the faucet finish first, then match hardware, lighting and mirror frames to it.
  • Mind the door swing. A smaller vanity or a pocket door can make a tight half bath feel twice the size.

Vanities & fixtures

Vanities, Fixtures & Storage

Stock or custom vanities, tops and sinks, plus faucets, mirrors, lighting and storage sized to the room.

  • Stock & custom vanities
  • Quartz, marble & solid-surface tops
  • Faucets, toilets & accessories
  • Vanity lighting & outlets
Planning guide: vanities
  • Measure the door, not just the wall. A vanity that fits the room still has to fit through the hallway and door.
  • Think about height. Comfort-height vanities (about 36 inches) are easier on your back than older 30-inch cabinets.
  • Light the face, not the top of the head. Sconces or a bar light at eye level beat a single ceiling fixture.

Local know-how

Remodeling Bathrooms in Trappe's Older and Newer Homes

Trappe is a borough of about 4,000 people with a long history: Augustus Lutheran Church dates to 1743, and the Speaker's House on West Main Street, once home to Frederick Muhlenberg, the first Speaker of the U.S. House, reopened in 2026 after a 25-year restoration. Homes range from early houses near Main Street to newer neighborhoods, and each kind comes with its own bathroom surprises.

Older homes near Main Street

Character worth keeping, plus a few things that only show up once the walls are open.

  • Uneven floors: we check the framing and flatten the floor before tile goes down.
  • Aging supply lines and drains: replaced while the walls are open, not patched later.
  • Plaster walls: cut cleanly and patched, or replaced with cement board where water will hit.
  • No real ventilation: a quiet exhaust fan ducted outdoors to protect the new room.

Newer neighborhoods

Solid houses with bathrooms that are often still original, and ready for an upgrade.

  • Fiberglass tub-shower units: replaced with a tiled shower or a tub-to-shower conversion.
  • Builder-grade vanities and cultured-marble tops: swapped for furniture-style vanities and quartz.
  • Fans venting into the attic: re-ducted to the outside so moisture leaves the house.
  • Dated lighting: new vanity lights at face height and GFCI outlets where code calls for them.

Trappe Borough permits

Trappe Borough builds under the Pennsylvania Uniform Construction Code, with plan reviews and inspections run through the borough's code office. Moving or adding a toilet, tub, shower, sink or drain needs a plumbing permit; swapping existing fixtures in the same spot generally doesn't. New electrical circuits can need a permit too. Applications include the contractor's certificate of insurance, and HOA approval if your neighborhood has an association. We confirm what your project needs, file the paperwork and schedule every inspection before the walls close.

Living at home during the work

Most Trappe homeowners stay put. We protect the floors and the path to the bathroom, put up a dust wall, haul debris out daily and tell you in the app which days the water will be off.

Trappe Bathroom Remodeling Questions

How much does a bathroom remodel cost in Trappe, PA?

Every bathroom is priced on its own. Your price depends on the size of the room, whether plumbing moves, and the tile, vanity and fixtures you choose. After a free on-site walkthrough you get a fixed, itemized estimate, so you can see exactly where the money goes and adjust before anything starts.

Can I use my own designer?

Yes. If you already work with a designer, we build from their layout and selections and work with them when questions come up on site. If you don't have one, we can connect you with one of our partner designers to plan the layout and choose tile, the vanity, fixtures and finishes. Already have plans? Bring them to your free walkthrough and we'll price your estimate from them.

Do I need a permit to remodel a bathroom in Trappe?

It depends on the work. Trappe Borough requires a plumbing permit when you move or add a toilet, tub, shower, sink or drain, or extend pipes for a new bathroom. Swapping existing fixtures in the same spot generally doesn't need one. New electrical circuits can need a permit too. We confirm the requirements with the borough for your project, file the applications and schedule the inspections.

Can you convert my tub to a walk-in shower?

Yes. Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common bathroom projects. We remove the tub, set a new shower pan, waterproof the walls and finish with tile, a niche and a glass door. Moving the drain usually means a plumbing permit, which we handle.

How long will my bathroom be out of commission?

Your estimate includes the schedule for your specific project. A refresh that keeps the plumbing where it is goes faster than a full remodel with layout changes and tile showers. Once work starts, your project app shows what was done each day and what's next.

Do you offer financing for bathroom remodels?

Yes, through Wisetack. Eligible homeowners can get 0% APR for up to 12 months, or a longer term with a lower monthly payment. Checking your options is a soft credit check and won't affect your credit score. All financing is subject to credit approval; see the terms on this page.

Do you work on older homes near Trappe's Main Street?

Yes. Older homes often hide uneven floors, plaster walls and aging supply lines. We check the floor framing before tile goes down, replace old supply lines while the walls are open, and keep details like trim profiles and wainscoting that suit the house.

Will the price change once you start?

Not without your signature. The price in your estimate only changes if you approve a written, priced change order.

What warranty do I get?

At closeout you receive a warranty certificate for our workmanship, with the term set in your contract, plus a product sheet listing the manufacturer, model and finish of everything we installed, so manufacturer warranties are easy to use.

Do you remodel bathrooms near Trappe too?

Yes. We're based in Linfield and remodel bathrooms across western Montgomery County, including Collegeville, Limerick, Royersford, Schwenksville, Skippack and Pottstown, plus King of Prussia and the rest of the county.
