        What to expect

        Your Free On-Site Estimate, Step by Step

        No pressure and no guesswork. Here's exactly what happens after you send your request.

        1. 1

          We set a time

          Our office reaches out by your preferred method to pick a time that works, including anyone who should be there to weigh in.

        2. 2

          We walk the space with you

          We measure, take photos and talk through what you want, what needs to stay, any HOA or building rules, and the budget you have in mind.

        3. 3

          You get an itemized estimate

          Every line item is listed, with clear payment terms. If you change the scope later, you sign a written, priced change order before that work happens.

        4. 4

          Design and samples, if you need them

          For remodels, our office manager works with you on layout, finishes and material samples before anything is ordered.

        5. 5

          Approve and meet your project manager

          Once you approve, a dedicated project manager schedules the work, handles permits and sends daily progress updates.

        Helpful to have ready

        • Photos or inspiration you like
        • A rough budget range
        • HOA, landlord or building rules
        • When you'd like the work done
        • Questions about financing

        Questions

        Estimate & Contact Questions

        Is the estimate really free?

        Yes. We come out, walk the space with you and send an itemized estimate at no charge.

        What happens after I send the form?

        Our office reaches out by your preferred method (call, text or email) to set up a time for your on-site estimate.

        Do you take commercial projects?

        Yes. We handle office renovations, tenant fit-outs, retail and storefront work, multifamily units, restrooms and building repairs, and can work after hours or on weekends. See commercial renovations.

        How do I claim a promotion?

        Choose it in the form, or tap an offer above. Promotions can't be combined. The Hometown Heroes offer requires a valid ID or proof of service.

        What areas do you serve?

        Our office is in Linfield, PA, and we work across Montgomery County, plus Philadelphia and Berks counties. See our service areas.

        Can I send photos?

        Yes. Mention it in the form and we'll send you a way to share photos before your estimate.
