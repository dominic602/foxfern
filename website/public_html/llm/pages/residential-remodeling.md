## Do I need a permit for my remodel?

Often, yes. In Pennsylvania, building permits are issued by your township or borough under the Uniform Construction Code. Layout changes, new electrical circuits, plumbing moves, basement finishing, decks and structural work usually need one; cosmetic updates often don't. Fox & Fern prepares the drawings, submits the application, schedules inspections and keeps the permit on site. Most attached decks need a permit, with inspections of the footings and the connection to the house.

## Who handles HOA approval?

Fox & Fern prepares the drawings, product specs and color samples your HOA's architectural review asks for, submits them and adjusts the plan if needed before work starts. Exterior changes like siding, roof color, decks and patios often need HOA review.

## How do I know if my stucco has water damage?

Look for stair-step or vertical cracks, brown streaks below windows and roof lines, white chalky deposits, and soft or bulging spots. Those signs often mean water is getting behind the surface. A proper repair starts by finding where the water enters, not just patching the crack. Fox & Fern repairs stucco across Montgomery County, Philadelphia and Berks County.

## Planning notes by project

- **Kitchens:** decide the layout first; cabinets usually set the schedule; stone countertops are templated after cabinets are installed; plan a temporary kitchen.
- **Bathrooms:** waterproofing behind the shower walls matters more than tile; vent the exhaust fan outside; pick fixtures early because shower valves go in before tile.
- **Basements:** solve moisture first; bedrooms need a code-compliant egress window or door; expect a permit and inspections.
- **Whole home:** removing or moving a wall can require a structural review; phasing decides which rooms you can use and when.
- **Concrete:** base and drainage decide lifespan; control joints help concrete crack where planned.
