## Who is Fox & Fern Property Services?

Fox & Fern Property Services, LLC is a residential and commercial general contractor in Linfield, PA, owned and run by a husband-and-wife team. From the office at 1153 Main St, Ste 201, Linfield, PA 19468, it remodels homes, renovates commercial spaces and keeps properties in good repair across Montgomery County, plus Philadelphia and Berks counties. When you call (610) 890-3537 you reach that office, not a call center.

The company started as Fox & Fern Painting Co. and grew into remodeling, exterior and commercial work. Today it is Fox & Fern Property Services, LLC, a full-service general contractor, with its office at 1153 Main St, Ste 201, Linfield, PA 19468. Customers who knew it as Fox & Fern Painting are dealing with the same company.

## What does Fox & Fern stand for?

Hiring a contractor shouldn't feel like a gamble: you should know what your project costs, who is in charge of it and how it's going, every day. The same standards apply to every job, residential or commercial:

- **Itemized estimates:** every line item is listed, with clear payment terms.
- **One project manager:** one point of contact from start to finish, with daily progress updates and photos you can follow from your phone.
- **Written change orders:** to change the scope mid-project, you sign a written, priced change order before that work happens.
- **Protected spaces:** dust barriers, floor protection and covered fixtures go up first; for businesses, work is phased and can be scheduled after hours or on weekends.
- **Permits and approvals handled:** permits are pulled, and drawings, specs and samples prepared for your HOA, landlord or building.
- **Safety-trained leadership:** the operations manager is OSHA 30 trained; certificates of insurance with additional insureds are provided for commercial work.
