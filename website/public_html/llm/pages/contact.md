## How do I request a free estimate from Fox & Fern?

Use the four-step form at [foxandfernservices.com/contact/](https://foxandfernservices.com/contact/) (about a minute) or call (610) 890-3537 (Monday to Friday 8 am to 6 pm, Saturday 9 am to 3 pm). Office visits at 1153 Main St, Ste 201, Linfield are by appointment. The steps are: property type (home, or business/property), the project (types of work, an optional promotion for homes, and when you'd like to start), the project address with optional details, and your contact details with your preferred contact method (call, text or email). The office then reaches out by that method to set up a time for the on-site estimate. Office: 1153 Main St, Ste 201, Linfield, PA 19468.

## Is the estimate really free?

Yes. Fox & Fern comes out, walks the space with you and sends an itemized estimate at no charge.

## What happens at the on-site estimate?

1. **A time is set** by your preferred contact method, including anyone who should be there to weigh in.
2. **The space is walked with you:** measurements, photos, what you want, what needs to stay, any HOA or building rules, and your budget.
3. **You get an itemized estimate:** every line item listed with clear payment terms. If you change the scope later, you sign a written, priced change order before that work happens.
4. **Design and samples, if needed:** for remodels, the office manager works with you on layout, finishes and material samples before anything is ordered.
5. **Approve and meet your project manager,** who schedules the work, handles permits and sends daily progress updates.

Helpful to have ready: photos or inspiration you like, a rough budget range, HOA, landlord or building rules, when you'd like the work done, and questions about financing.

## How does 0% APR financing work?

Homeowners with residential projects can pay over time through Wisetack, with 0% APR for up to 24 months for eligible customers. Check your options from your phone in a few minutes; prequalifying is a soft credit check, so it doesn't affect your credit score. If you move forward with a plan, the lender may run a full credit check. Commercial projects are billed on the payment terms in the bid. Financing can't be combined with other promotions. Full disclosure: [financing terms](https://foxandfernservices.com/llm/pages/home.md#what-promotions-are-available).

## How do I claim a promotion?

Choose it in the form (residential requests) or tap an offer on the page. Promotions can't be combined. Current offers: 0% APR for up to 24 months through Wisetack;* 20% off concrete booked Oct 1, 2026 – Feb 28, 2027 for spring 2027 installation; and 15% off for Hometown Heroes (veterans, first responders, teachers and nurses; valid ID or proof of service required).

\*All financing is subject to credit approval. Your terms may vary.
