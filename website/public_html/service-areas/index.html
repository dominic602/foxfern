Questions

Service Area Questions

Where is Fox & Fern located?

Our office is at 1153 Main St, Ste 201, Linfield, PA 19468, in Limerick Township, Montgomery County.

What areas do you serve?

Montgomery County, plus Philadelphia and Berks counties, including Limerick, Royersford, Pottstown, Collegeville, King of Prussia, Willow Grove, Bryn Mawr, Philadelphia and Boyertown.

Have you worked outside these towns?

Yes. Our completed projects also include Lansdale, Conshohocken and Gilbertsville in Montgomery County, and Reading in Berks County. See them on our projects page. For an address outside our usual area, send it in the estimate form and we'll let you know right away.

What work do you do in these towns?

The same in every town: kitchen, bathroom, basement and whole-home remodeling, decks, siding, stucco and concrete; commercial renovations for offices, retail and multifamily buildings; and handyman, plumbing and electrical repairs and maintenance.

Do you work on commercial properties in these towns?

Yes. We handle office renovations, tenant fit-outs, retail, multifamily units, restrooms and building repairs, and can schedule work after hours or on weekends. See commercial renovations.

Is the estimate free everywhere you work?

Yes. We come out, walk the space with you and send an itemized estimate at no charge.
