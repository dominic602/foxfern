What to expect

What to Expect at Your Estimate

No pressure and no guesswork. Here's how your visit works, and what happens after.

  1. 01

    You're on the calendar

    Your free on-site estimate is booked, and we'll see you then. If anyone else should weigh in, like a spouse, a property manager or your designer, plan to have them there.

  2. 02

    We walk the space with you

    We measure, take photos and talk through what you want, what needs to stay, any HOA or building rules, and the budget you have in mind. No pressure.

  3. 03

    You get an itemized estimate

    Every line item is listed, with clear payment terms, so you can see exactly where the money goes. If you change the scope later, you sign a written, priced change order before that work happens.

  4. 04

    Design and samples, if you need them

    For remodels, Melissa works with you on layout, finishes and material samples before anything is ordered. Already working with a designer? Bring them in.

  5. 05

    Approve and meet your project manager

    Once you approve, a dedicated project manager schedules the work, handles permits and sends daily progress updates to your phone.

Helpful to have ready

  • Photos or inspiration you like
  • A rough budget range, so we can suggest options that fit
  • HOA, landlord or building rules
  • When you'd like the work done
  • Anyone who should weigh in
  • Questions about financing

Financing through Wisetack

0% APR for Up to 24 Months

We've upgraded our financing: eligible homeowners can now spread the cost over up to 24 months at 0% APR.* Ask about it at your walkthrough, or check your options from your phone.

  1. 1

    Check your options

    Apply from your phone in a few minutes. Prequalifying is a soft credit check, so it doesn't affect your credit score.

  2. 2

    Choose a plan

    Pick the term and monthly payment that fit your budget. If you move forward with a plan, the lender may run a full credit check.

  3. 3

    Start your project

    We schedule the work and keep you updated every day. You make your monthly payments through Wisetack.
