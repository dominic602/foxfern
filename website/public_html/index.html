How it works

Our Process

A clear price, one project manager and daily updates, so you never have to guess where your project stands.

  1. 01

    On-site walkthrough

    We walk the whole space with you, take measurements and talk through exactly what you want.

  2. 02

    Itemized estimate

    A clear, itemized estimate with a fixed price and written payment terms. It only changes if you sign a change order.

  3. 03

    Design & samples, if needed

    Renderings, material sourcing and samples, so you can see it before we build it.

  4. 04

    Your dedicated project manager

    Once you approve, a dedicated project manager takes over, focused entirely on your experience: clear communication and daily quality control.

  5. 05

    Build, with daily updates

    Work starts on schedule. Your project app is updated every day with what was done, photos and what's next.

  6. 06

    Final walkthrough & closeout

    We walk the finished work together and complete every touch-up. You get warranty certificates and a product sheet listing the exact products used.
